Optional offline translation models are not bundled with SnapCut. When the
user confirms a download, the model files are fetched directly from Mozilla's
official Firefox Translations model registry:
https://storage.googleapis.com/moz-fx-translations-data--303e-prod-translations-data/db/models.json

The optional PP-OCRv6 model files, Qwen2.5 model, and llama.cpp runtime are
also not bundled. They are downloaded only after the user explicitly starts
the corresponding download in settings. Their source and license information
is listed above.

The notices and license files of the projects above remain available from
their source repositories. SnapCut does not claim ownership of those projects
or model files.
